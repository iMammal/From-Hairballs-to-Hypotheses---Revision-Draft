# Proposed manuscript synthesis

## Section 3 / Figure 2 lead-in

A targeted full-report assessment of the three machine-coded cells with no supported representative did not justify relaxing either axis. In the assessed reports, desktop and wall configurations frequently co-occurred with three-dimensional rendering, stereoscopy, or direct manipulation, but these properties did not by themselves show that computation used spatial or embodied context. Likewise, the only accessible Adaptive × Large Display candidate implemented its adaptive mechanism in a CAVE rather than on a large-display workflow. The corresponding Figure 2 cells therefore remain “Representative not yet established,” meaning not represented among assessed eligible systems rather than absent from the literature.

## Section 5 boundary comparison

The closest desktop case, StarmapVis, makes the locus of agency unusually explicit: the analyst selects a single-cell trajectory branch, the browser camera executes a path derived from the trajectory geometry, and the analyst interprets and can redirect the resulting tour \cite{ma2023starmapvis}. The target mechanism is implemented in non-VR mode, but the system remains outside the supported count because its high-precision eligibility is unresolved: the report primarily visualizes outputs generated by upstream pipelines. MinOmics presents the complementary large-display boundary. Its molecular viewer implements atom-conditioned viewpoint and camera-path computation, yet the reported wall workflows use mouse or gyroscopic direct manipulation and do not demonstrate that guided navigation in the wall analysis \cite{maes2018minomics}. These cases separate evidence for a mechanism from evidence that the mechanism operates in the claimed modality.
