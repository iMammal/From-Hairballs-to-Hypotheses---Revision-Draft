# Concise author-decision list

The evidence ledger is automated and pending author approval. These are the decisions that materially affect the synthesis; no record-by-record approval is requested.

1. **Vitessce Link, Immersive × AR/MR.** The automated assessment treats registered cross-device selection and gesture-based spatial interaction as computational use of spatial context, not merely MR display. Approve or move this one cell to unresolved. Evidence: `N012`, X015 PDF p. 7 and p. 12.

2. **Planning versus procedural guidance.** The assessment counts patient-specific systems when segmentation, registration, reconstruction, or cohort computation is used for relational preoperative analysis, but leaves biopsy guidance, needle navigation, telepresence, and education contextual or unresolved. Approve this boundary as a group; examples and locators are in the eligibility and placement matrices.

3. **moleculARweb eligibility.** The implemented kits support molecular comparison and simplified mechanics, but the reports emphasize education and characterize some research use as small-scale or prospective. Decide whether that satisfies E1--E5 for an eligible analytical workflow. Current automated status: eligible, Algorithmic × Desktop/Planar and AR/MR. Evidence: `N007`--`N008`, especially X128 PDF pp. 1, 3--5.

4. **Interactive Biohybrid Simulations eligibility.** The report implements agent-based plant/robot development simulations and interactive VR/AR design, but the use case straddles life-science analysis and biohybrid engineering design. Current automated status: eligible; Immersive × AR/MR remains unresolved. Evidence: `N005`--`N006` and `N052`, X010 PDF pp. 1--2 and 10.

5. **Conversational desktop cluster.** CellWhisperer, Robin, scSelector, i-gRINN, REDAC, and CERA are counted because natural language mediates analytical operations or explanations. 6. **iCAVE identity and boundary.** X008 is a related preprint for the already assessed published iCAVE system. It adds no system or Algorithmic-cell count. The accepted rejection of Immersive × CAVE is retained because tracking changes perspective/interaction, not the network-analysis computation.

7. **VR–AR medical-image analysis preprint.** X139 describes implemented reconstruction and rendering algorithms but offers limited evidence of an analyst evaluation. Current automated status: eligible for Algorithmic × VR and AR/MR capability, with no evaluated-benefit claim. Approve or move to unresolved if the implementation evidence is judged insufficient.
