# Proposed two-panel diagram structure

## Panel A — Identification and machine-assisted title/abstract screening

1. **Sources identified:** 187,446 occurrences, with the nine registered retrieval/import routes listed inside or beside the box.
2. **Identity consolidation:** 140,959 canonical records available for screening; annotate 46,487 consolidated occurrence surplus and the DOI-first/title-fallback rule.
3. **Terminal screening-state split (additive):**
   - 140,810 valid effective screenings;
   - 35 technical failures;
   - 4 ambiguous requests not resubmitted;
   - 110 protected evaluation/calibration records;
   - 0 genuinely unprocessed.
4. **Valid-outcome split (additive):** 9,505 ADVANCE, 83,258 DEFER, 48,047 EXCLUDED.
5. **Nested annotation:** 10,460 background designations are a subset of EXCLUDED.
6. **Candidate handoff:** 9,505 unique categorized machine-coded candidate records. Note that coding is descriptive, not eligibility.

## Panel B — Purposeful selection and full-report synthesis

1. **Purposeful multiwave selection:** 384 selected report-identity rows representing 381 systems. Show six wave labels in a side table or note, not as a false sequential funnel.
2. **Report access split (report unit, additive):** 135 accessible full-report rows, 1 abstract-only row, 248 unavailable report rows.
3. **System assessment split (system unit, additive):** 132 completed system assessments and 249 systems without completed full-report assessment.
4. **Completed-assessment outcomes (additive):** 80 eligible, 19 unresolved, 33 excluded/contextual.
5. **Map coverage among eligible systems (additive):** 76 with supported placements and 4 without supported placements.
6. **Multilabel map expansion:** 118 supported system–cell placements from 76 systems; use a non-additive connector.

## Connection between panels

Use a dashed, explicitly non-exhaustive arrow from the 9,505-candidate box to the multiwave selection box. Label it: “purposeful coverage selection; 373/381 systems exact-ID-linked to candidate pool; eight additional coauthor/map-expansion-route systems.” Do not imply that all 381 systems are a direct subset of the candidate pool, and do not label 9,505 as included studies.
