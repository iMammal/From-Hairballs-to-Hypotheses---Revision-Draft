Dear Editors,

Please consider our revised State-of-the-Art Report, ``From Hairballs to Hypotheses: A Survey of AI-Assisted Visual Analytics in the Life Sciences,'' for publication in \textit{Computer Graphics Forum}.

We thank the editors and reviewers for their thoughtful feedback. The revision strengthens the manuscript's comparative synthesis around the \textbf{locus of analytic agency}: how scientists and computational systems share initiative and control over analytical actions. Our Assistance $\times$ Visualization Modality framework connects these differences to five scientific task groups and examines their implications for interpretation, verification, and evaluation.

We have expanded and clarified the review methodology, documenting search and screening procedures, purpose-specific system selection, full-text access, and the evidence supporting framework placements. The revised synthesis draws on 135 completed system assessments, with 79 systems supporting 122 assistance--modality placements. Throughout the manuscript, we distinguish implemented mechanisms, proposed uses, and evaluated findings, and make the limits of the available evidence explicit.

The accompanying methodology supplement provides detailed accounting and per-system evidence. The reproducibility materials include protocols, audit exports, evidence tables, and scripts for recomputing the reported aggregates. A separate response letter explains how we have addressed the reviewers' comments.

We believe these revisions provide a clearer and more useful account of how computational assistance participates in life-science visual analysis, what current evaluations establish, and which design and evaluation questions remain open.

Thank you for considering our revised manuscript.
