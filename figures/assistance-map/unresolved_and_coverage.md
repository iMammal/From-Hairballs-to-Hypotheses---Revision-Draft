# Unresolved placements and coverage gaps

- The current integrated revision manuscript file is unavailable. Exact existing citation keys, section labels, and surrounding prose cannot be reconciled until that specific post-submission manuscript source or PDF is supplied.
- Comparative Pathology Workbench remains contextual with E4 unresolved. It is not an eligible matrix example; PeCaX remains the replacement.
- iCAVE supports Algorithmic × CAVE and Algorithmic × Desktop/Planar. Immersive × CAVE remains unresolved because the same algorithms operate outside the CAVE and the report does not show the assistance changing with tracked immersive context.
- Talk to the Wall is a useful contextual large-display collaboration study, but its fixed speech commands duplicate touch operations and the task is non-life-science. It is not a Conversational placement.
- The Exocentric/Egocentric VR report is useful biomedical evaluation context, but VR presentation and viewpoint comparison do not establish a substantive assistance mechanism.
- RetainVis is not placed as Adaptive in this map: the assessed report supports user-directed edits and retraining, but not system-selected tailoring from behavior, context, or inferred intent.
- No assessed example establishes Adaptive assistance on Large Display, VR, AR/MR, or CAVE; Conversational assistance on Large Display, VR, AR/MR, or CAVE; or Immersive assistance on Desktop/Planar, Large Display, or CAVE.
- These are evidence-coverage gaps in the bounded assessed set. They are not claims that no corresponding systems or literature exist.
- No quantitative density, bubble size, heat intensity, prevalence, or denominator is encoded. The 9,505 machine-coded candidates were used only for identity lookup, not as validated evidence or a denominator.
